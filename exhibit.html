The 19th International Symposium on Visual Information Communication and Interaction (VINCI 2026)

Hangzhou, China, 1-4 November 2026

Exhibit

Art & Media Gallery · Futures Unbound

OōEli Tianmuli

OōEli Tianmuli is a landmark international art complex in Hangzhou and the official exhibition venue for the Art and Media Gallery Track of VINCI 2026 (the 19th International Symposium on Visual Information Communication and Interaction). Featuring minimalist open spaces, natural lighting, and professional audio-visual & exhibition support systems, the venue fully supports new media and tech-art displays, including AI co-creation, XR immersive installations, data visualization, and interactive digital works. With rich experience in hosting international art and academic exhibitions, Tianmuli advocates inclusive and participatory public art, which closely aligns with VINCI 2026’s core vision of integrating technology, art, and cross-cultural communication.
