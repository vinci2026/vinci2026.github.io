Conference Venue

VINCI 2026 will be held at Hangzhou Chengbei Wenlan Hotel. The hotel is located in Gongshu District, close to Chengbei Joy City and approximately 400 metres from Beidaqiao Metro Station.

Location

Gongshu District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China
